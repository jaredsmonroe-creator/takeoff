Take Off Tool

  1. Tap 📄 Open Plan and pick the PDF (Files or Google Drive).
  2. Pick the page, then tap 📏 Set Scale.
  3. Tap 📐 Check and measure a printed dimension to verify the scale.
  4. Tap a + button (Roof, Walls, Garage…) and tap the corners on the plan.
  5. Tap 💾 Save to send the CSV to Files / Google Drive for Tess.

One finger drag = move the plan. Two fingers = zoom. Quick tap = place a point.
